# Introduction

This project explores stock trends in the semiconductor industry — a world full of chips, charts, and constant change. Starting with data from 2015 to 2023, I analysed key players like NVIDIA, Intel, AMD, Qualcomm, and TSM, looking at returns, volatility, and how their prices move together.

Why semiconductors? Because they’re at the heart of modern tech — and I find the industry fascinating. This is just the beginning: I plan to expand the project with more data, deeper insights, and new ways to understand how this fast-moving sector behaves.
